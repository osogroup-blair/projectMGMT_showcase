Tab Requirements (what each tab must contain)

- Planned vs actual comparison (scope/effort/delivery)

- Team/stakeholder feedback

- Retained for trend analysis across sprints

Purpose: Configure sprint behavior and governance.
Includes:
- Sprint status (planned, active, closed)
