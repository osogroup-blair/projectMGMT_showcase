# Sprint Insights Tab — Product Spec

This document defines the purpose, behavior, and structure of the **Sprint Insights** tab.  
This tab is focused on **decision-making, sprint health, and safe sprint closure**, not day-to-day execution.

---

## 🎯 Core Job of the Sprint Insights Tab

> **“Help me understand how the sprint is going, decide what to do next, and safely close it.”**

This page must clearly answer:
- Are we on track?
- What’s blocked or unfinished?
- What happens to leftover work?
- Is this sprint ready to close?

This is a **reflection and decision surface**, not an execution surface.

---

## 1️⃣ Sprint Close & Backlog Rollover (Primary Feature)

### A) Close Sprint Action
- Primary CTA: **Close Sprint**
- Must be clearly visible and intentional
- Not hidden behind menus

---

### B) Sprint Close Flow (Required)

#### Step 1 — Sprint Summary Review (Auto-generated)
Show a read-only summary:
- Sprint name and dates
- Sprint goal
- Task counts:
  - Completed
  - In Progress
  - Not Started
  - Blocked
- Completion percentage

Purpose: confirm what actually happened before closing.

---

#### Step 2 — Backlog Rollover (Critical)
For all unfinished tasks, present options:

- ✅ Move to next sprint (**default**)
- ⏸ Move to project backlog (unscheduled)
- ❌ Close / cancel task (optional, gated)
- 🔁 Reassign to another sprint (optional v1.1)

Behavior:
- Default selection = Move to next sprint
- Bulk selection supported
- Show task counts per choice

This step ensures **no work is lost**.

---

#### Step 3 — Confirm & Execute
- Final confirmation summary:
  - “X tasks moved to Sprint N+1”
  - “Y tasks moved to backlog”
- Close sprint atomically
- Optionally auto-create next sprint if none exists

---

### C) After Close Behavior
- Sprint status becomes **Closed**
- Run tab becomes read-only
- Insights tab becomes historical
- Redirect user to:
  - Next sprint, or
  - Sprint list

---

## 2️⃣ Sprint Health & Progress (Before Closing)

### A) Sprint Progress Overview
Show:
- Sprint goal
- Progress bar (% tasks completed)
- Days elapsed vs total sprint duration
- Velocity proxy (simple):
  - Tasks completed / tasks planned

---

### B) Completion Breakdown
Show a clear breakdown:
- Done
- In Progress
- Not Started
- Blocked

Each category is clickable to drill into tasks.

---

### C) Risk & Signals (Actionable Only)
Show:
- 🚨 Blocked tasks count
- ⚠️ Overdue tasks
- 🔄 Tasks untouched for N days
- 📉 Sprint slipping indicator (rule-based)

Avoid heavy charts; show only signals that require action.

---

## 3️⃣ Additional Content That Belongs Here

### A) Scope Changes (Optional but Valuable)
If task history exists:
- Tasks added mid-sprint
- Tasks removed mid-sprint

This explains why outcomes differ from the plan.

---

### B) Sprint Notes / Retrospective Seed
Optional lightweight notes section:
- Free-text “Sprint Notes”
- Used to capture:
  - What went well
  - What didn’t
  - Context for future retros

Not a full retro tool.

---

### C) Historical Snapshot (After Close)
Once closed:
- All data becomes read-only
- Acts as a permanent record of sprint outcome
- Supports future analysis and retrospectives

---

## 4️⃣ What Should NOT Be on This Page

Do NOT include:
- Drag-and-drop boards
- Task editing
- Async standups (Run tab responsibility)
- Heavy analytics or charts
- Time tracking details

If it’s about *doing work*, it does not belong here.
