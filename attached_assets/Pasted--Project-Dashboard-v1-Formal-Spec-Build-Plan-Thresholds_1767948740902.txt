# Project Dashboard v1 — Formal Spec, Build Plan & Thresholds

This document defines a **time-horizon–based Project Dashboard** designed to answer:
- What do I need to get done **this week**?
- What’s coming **next week**?
- Are we **on track over the next 1–3 months**?

The dashboard is **signal-first**, not a task dump.

---

## 🎯 North Star
“Tell me what matters now, what’s coming soon, and where risks are forming — without me hunting.”

---

## Primary Users
- **Project Lead / PM** – trajectory, milestones, risks
- **Contributor / IC** – personal commitments and priorities
- **Manager** (optional filter) – cross-project visibility

---

## Information Architecture

### Global Controls (persist per user)
- Time Range: This Week (default) | Next Week | 30 / 60 / 90 Days
- Project Scope: All projects or selected projects
- Ownership:
  - Assigned to me
  - My team
  - Everyone
- Optional (v1.1): Work Type (Planning / Execution / Review)
- Search: task / milestone keyword

---

## Page Layout
1. **This Week** — Execution Mode  
2. **Next Week** — Preparation Mode  
3. **Next 1–3 Months** — Trajectory Mode  

Each section includes:
- Summary bar (counts + warnings)
- 2–4 panels with drill-down

---

# Phase 1 — Data & API Foundation

## Dashboard Aggregation Endpoint
GET /api/dashboard

### Query Params
- range: week | nextWeek | 30days | 60days | 90days
- projectIds[] (optional)
- assigneeScope: me | team | all

### Response Shape
summary:
- tasksDue: number
- overdue: number
- blocked: number
- milestonesDue: number
- capacityLevel: low | medium | high

thisWeek:
- myCommitments: Task[]
- atRisk: Task[]
- weeklyFocus: ProjectFocus[]

nextWeek:
- milestones: Milestone[]
- rollingTasks: Task[]
- capacityPreview: CapacityStats

future:
- milestones: Milestone[]
- stageProgress: StageSummary[]
- risks: RiskItem[]

**Rules**
- Scoring and filtering happen server-side
- Limit each panel to top 10–15 items

---

# Phase 2 — Priority & Risk Logic

## Priority Score (used for ordering)
- Overdue: +50
- Due in 0–2 days: +30
- Due in 3–7 days: +20
- Due in 8–14 days: +10
- Blocks ≥1 task: +15
- Blocks ≥5 tasks: +25
- Linked to milestone due ≤7 days: +15
- Not started & due ≤7 days: +10
- In progress: +5

---

## At-Risk Task Rules
A task is **At Risk** if ANY apply:
- Overdue
- Due in ≤2 days and not In Progress/Done
- Blocked
- No activity ≥7 days and due ≤7 days
- Long-running (created ≥30 days ago and not done)

Sorted by **risk score** (priority score capped).

# Phase 3 — This Week (Execution Mode)

## Summary Bar
- Tasks due this week
- Overdue tasks
- Blocked tasks
- Milestones due this week

---

## My Commitments
**Include**
- Tasks due this week
- Scoped by assignee filter

**Display**
- Task name
- Project
- Due date (days left)
- Status
- Priority indicator
- Flags: Overdue / Blocked / Blocking

**Limit**
- Top 15 + “View all”

---

## At Risk
- Top 10 highest risk tasks
- Grouped by project (collapsible)

---

## Weekly Focus (Per Project)
- Up to 3 items per project

**Selection Logic**
1. Milestone due this week → milestone + critical tasks
2. Else → top priority tasks due this week

---

# Phase 4 — Next Week (Preparation Mode)

## Summary Bar
- Tasks due next week
- Milestones starting next week
- Milestones due next week
- Capacity warning

---

## Upcoming Milestones
**Include**
- Milestones starting OR due next week

**Confidence Rules**
- At Risk if:
  - Linked task overdue
  - Remaining tasks > 10 and due ≤7 days
  - Milestone due ≤7 days and no tasks in progress

---

## Tasks Rolling In
**Include**
- Tasks due next week
- Tasks dependent on this week’s tasks

**Grouping**
- Default: by project
- Optional toggle: by day

---

## Capacity Preview (Lightweight)
No hours, counts only.

**Thresholds**
- Low: ≤5 not-started tasks
- Medium: 6–10
- High: >10

---

# Phase 5 — Next 1–3 Months (Trajectory Mode)

## Milestone Timeline
- Milestones within 30/60/90 days
- Grouped by project
- Max 20 items, paginate if needed

---

## Stage Progress
For each project:
- Current stage
- Stage start/end
- Days remaining
- Health indicator

**At Risk**
- Stage end ≤14 days and open tasks >10
- OR stage duration exceeded by 20%

---

## Risk Radar
Includes:
- Long-running tasks (≥30 days open)
- High blockers (blocking ≥5 tasks)
- Slipping milestones (date changed ≥2 times if history exists)

---

# Phase 6 — UX & Interaction

## Global UX Rules
- Read-first dashboard
- No bulk editing
- Click → detail view
- “View all” deep-links to filtered lists
- Never render >15 items per panel

---

# Phase 7 — Performance & Safety

## Performance Targets
- First meaningful render <1s (warm cache)
- React Query caching
- Server-side pagination

---

## Notifications
- Dashboard does not emit notifications
- No toasts on load
- Purely informational
